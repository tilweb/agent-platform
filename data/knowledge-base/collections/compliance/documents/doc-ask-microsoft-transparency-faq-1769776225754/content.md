• What is the Ask Microsoft?

Ask Microsoft is an AI-powered chatbot built on Microsoft Copilot to answer questions about
Microsoft’s products and services on Microsoft websites where it is available for use.

• What can Ask Microsoft do?

The AI assistant (or chatbot) is designed to provide helpful information about products and
services on the respective website where the chatbot is integrated. The chatbot generates an
answer using Microsoft Copilot Studio capabilities using Microsoft websites as a data source.

• What is Ask Microsoft’s intended use?

Allow website visitors to easily find answers using natural language questions.

• How was Ask Microsoft evaluated? What metrics are used to measure performance?
Red teaming and manual reviews of hundreds of questions and the generated AI answers.

The transcripts and feedback are stored for analytics and may be used for improving the quality
of the chatbot or Microsoft products and services.

• What are the limitations of Ask Microsoft? How can users minimize the impact of Ask

Microsoft’s limitations when using the system?
The chatbot uses AI to generate an answer for the user prompts. AI-generated content may be
incorrect. Please make sure to read through the citation links provided along with the AI response
or contact the sales team for more information by prompting “chat with sales”. The chatbot is
primarily trained on English (U.S.) text. Any other language or English variations may not be
accurate.

•

For another question or to report an issue about the chatbot:
Type “Submit feedback” in the chatbot and follow the prompts to provide information. Please do
not submit any personal information in your responses. If the chatbot itself is not working
properly for you, please contact Microsoft support.

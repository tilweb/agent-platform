### User
Warum ist der Himmel blau?

### Assistant
Der Himmel erscheint blau aufgrund eines Phänomens namens Rayleigh-Streuung. Wenn Sonnenlicht in die Erdatmosphäre eintritt, trifft es auf die Gasmoleküle und winzige Partikel in der Luft. Shorter wavelengths of light, like blue and violet, are scattered more than other colors because they travel in shorter, smaller waves. This is why we perceive the sky as blue most of the time. During sunrise or sunset, the sky can appear red or orange because the light has to pass through more of the atmosphere, scattering the blue light away and leaving the red and orange hues.
